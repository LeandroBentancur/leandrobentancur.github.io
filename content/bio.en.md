I work in applied algebraic geometry, where real algebraic geometry, approximation
theory and optimization meet. With Mauricio Velasco and Didier Henrion I introduced
mollified Christoffel–Darboux kernels on algebraic varieties, which recover a density
without requiring the equilibrium measure of its support.

My second line is the Fekete problem: how to place *N* points on a sphere so that their
logarithmic energy is extremal. For *N* = 12 the answer is the icosahedron — one of the
few cases known exactly, and the mark at the top of this page.
